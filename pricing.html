How it works

Three steps from rate to delivered

🔑

1. Create a free account

Sign up with your email or Google. No fee, no card, no subscription — the account is what your orders are tracked against.

🧮

2. Pick a service & quantity

Open the boost panel, choose the service, paste the link and enter the quantity. The amount in naira calculates instantly from the rate per 1,000.

⏳

3. We approve & deliver

The amount is deducted from your wallet when you submit. We review the order and it moves to approved, in progress and completed; rejected orders are refunded.

Your dashboard shows every order with its status, quantity, rate and amount — plus the link that was boosted.
