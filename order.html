Good to know

Boosting questions, answered

Most orders are reviewed within a few hours during working days (Mon–Fri, 9am–6pm WAT). Orders submitted overnight are picked up first thing the next morning.
Our team assigns a processing priority to every service — Urgent, High, Normal or Low. It decides the order your job is worked on when several are in the queue. Urgent services are delivered first; low priority services are used to fill capacity at a lower price.
Nothing is charged when you submit. After review we confirm the final price and send payment details (bank transfer, card or Paystack). Work starts once payment is confirmed and the order is marked ongoing.
